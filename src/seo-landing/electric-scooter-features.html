Electric Scooter Features

Choosing an electric scooter involves understanding more than its design. Equipment, battery configuration and available features also matter. Explore features listed in the ARTH.Pravah Aero and Apex product catalogues.

LED Headlamp with DRL

The catalogue lists LED headlamp lighting with daytime running lights. Confirm the supplied lighting configuration for the selected model.

Disc Braking

Disc braking is listed in the product catalogue. Aero and Apex list a front disc brake. Confirm the complete braking configuration for your vehicle.

USB Charging

A USB charging connection is listed for small devices. Confirm port rating and device compatibility before use.

Reverse Mode

Reverse mode is listed as a catalogue feature. Ask the team to demonstrate its operation and explain operating instructions before use.

Anti-Theft Alarm

An anti-theft alarm is listed in the catalogue. Remote centre lock and start button are also listed. Confirm which equipment is included.

LED Display, Telescopic Suspension and Alloy Rims

An LED display is listed in the product catalogue. Telescopic suspension and alloy rims are listed in Aero and Apex catalogue information. Confirm the supplied configuration.

Battery Options

Aero and Apex catalogue pages list lithium-ion and lead-acid VRLA battery configurations. Charging times, capacity, range and warranty depend on the selected configuration.
